# FirstBuck — Plan

## Problem

Indie makers sit on rough ideas for months. Building feels productive; selling feels scary. They need a **tiny, concrete offer** they can list today — not a pitch deck or a 12-week course.

## Audience

- Solo founders / indie hackers with notes-app ideas
- Freelancers packaging expertise into digital products
- Builders who already have WIP or a shipped tool and need a $1–$9 wedge

## Why $1 (for FirstBuck itself)

- Impulse buy → converts curiosity into paid users
- Matches the product thesis (“micro-offers”)
- Enough to validate willingness-to-pay and cover Stripe fees + coffee
- Upsells later: packs, templates, Pro regenerate, team seats

## Competition

| Alternative | Gap |
|-------------|-----|
| ChatGPT prompts | No productized flow, no unlock, inconsistent quality |
| Offer / landing builders | Overkill; optimized for $49–$499 courses |
| Notion templates | Static; no generation from *your* idea |
| “Just ship” Twitter advice | Motivation ≠ artifacts |

FirstBuck wins on **speed + specificity + sellable artifacts**.

## Monetization

1. **Free:** 1 generation, watermark, no Markdown, Gumroad section locked
2. **$1 unlock:** full pack, Markdown download, remove mark, 1 regenerate
3. **Later:** $9 “Launch week” pack, affiliates, bulk credits

## GTM (first 7 days)

1. Post on X with a screen recording of idea → pack
2. IndieHackers “I launched” + ask for feedback on promise clarity
3. Reddit r/SideProject + r/EntrepreneurRideAlong (value-first)
4. DM 20 makers who tweeted “I have an idea but…”
5. Soft PH launch once Stripe live + custom domain

## Metrics

- Landing → Create CTR
- Generations / day
- Unlock conversion rate (goal: ≥5% of generators)
- Time to first $1 (activation story)
- Qualitative: “Did you list anything within 24h?”

## Risks

- Stripe not configured → no revenue (mitigated by OPEN_ITEMS)
- Template copy feels generic → iterate phrases from real user ideas
- Abuse / unlimited free gens → rate-limit or fingerprint later
- Refunds / chargebacks on $1 → keep friction low, deliver instantly
